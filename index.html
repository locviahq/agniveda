MORE THAN JUST GHEE

Some traditions
deserve to be preserved.

Before it became a product, it was a part of our everyday life.

In our home, the art of making ghee begins with milk from our own family-owned animals. Through the traditional curd-making and bilona process, we carry forward a way of life shaped by patience, care and generations of tradition.

AGNIVEDA is our way of bringing that tradition closer to your home.
