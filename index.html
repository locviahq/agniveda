धैर्य  /  patience

Why AGNIVEDA

Good ghee cannot be hurried.

AGNIVEDA comes from Agni, the flame, and Veda, knowledge handed down. It is a village tradition brought to modern homes, without shortcuts.

Milk becomes curd. Curd is churned into butter. Butter is warmed slowly until it turns into ghee. Each step takes time, and we do not skip any of it.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is bilona ghee?

Bilona is a traditional method. Milk is set into curd, the curd is churned to separate butter, and the butter is slowly heated to make ghee.

Where does your milk come from?

From our family's own cows and buffaloes.
