Our own animals
Where it begins

The ghee starts with our family's animals.

Every jar of AGNIVEDA begins long before the kitchen, in the care we give our own cows and buffaloes and the fresh milk they give us.

No middlemen between the animal and the ghee. Just a family, a tradition, and the patience to do it the old way.

The journey

Nine careful steps from milk to golden ghee.

Every step is done by hand, in order, and given the time it needs.

1

Milk collection

Fresh milk from our own animals.

2

Milk preparation

Gently readied for setting.

3

Curd setting

Left to ferment slowly.

4

Bilona churning

Curd churned the traditional way.

5

Fresh makkhan

White butter separates and is collected.

6

Slow heating

Makkhan is warmed on a gentle flame.

7

Golden separation

Golden ghee parts from the milk solids.

8

Filtering

Strained and prepared for the jar.

9

The final ghee

Ready to reach your kitchen.
